Gentle Yoga

Gentle Yoga is a slower class with simple movements and stretches. It is a good choice for beginners and older adults.

Vinyasa Yoga

Vinyasa Yoga connects movement and breathing. Students can choose easier options when needed.

Restorative Yoga

Restorative Yoga uses restful poses and slow breathing to help students relax.
