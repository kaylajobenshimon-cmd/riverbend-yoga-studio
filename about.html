Accessibility

Riverbend Yoga Studio offers chair options, space for mobility devices, and pose changes when needed.

Studio Policies

  • Arrive a few minutes before class.
  • Wear comfortable clothing.
  • Tell your instructor about injuries or mobility needs.
  • Rest whenever you need to.
  • Silence phones during class.
